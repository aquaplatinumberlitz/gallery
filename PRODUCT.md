# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Local AI image/video collectors and maintainers who browse, inspect, search, and manage personal generated-media libraries on desktop, tablet, and mobile. The admin surfaces are used during library setup, scan maintenance, and catalog troubleshooting.

## Product Purpose

AI Art Gallery is a local-first gallery and catalog tool for registered AI-generated media libraries. It should make large local collections fast to browse, easy to inspect, and safe to maintain without moving or deleting source files unexpectedly.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, technical. The interface should feel like a trustworthy local tool: dense enough for repeat workflows, restrained in styling, and clear about filesystem effects.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-site ornament, decorative dashboards, over-bright accent colors, and modal UI that hides important filesystem consequences. Avoid whimsical or playful treatments around destructive or scan-related actions.

## Design Principles

- Keep filesystem actions explicit and reversible where possible.
- Favor task clarity over visual novelty.
- Preserve density for power-user workflows without making errors hard to scan.
- Put validation and status messages next to the data they explain.
- Use familiar product UI patterns from the existing shadcn-vue-style component system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic form structure, visible labels, keyboard-accessible controls, and clear invalid states. Preserve reduced-motion compatibility and theme-token contrast across light and dark modes.
